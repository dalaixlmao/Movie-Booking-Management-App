# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 7, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the Movie Booking App ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our movie booking service.

This policy applies to information we collect:
- Through our website and mobile application
- When you register an account
- When you make movie ticket bookings
- When you contact our customer support
- Through cookies and tracking technologies

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using our service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies, please do not use our service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide

We collect information you provide directly to us when using our service:

- **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and password.
- **Profile Information**: You may optionally provide your city, state, and ZIP code.
- **Payment Information**: When you make a booking, we process payment details such as credit/debit card information. Note: We do not store complete payment card details on our servers.
- **Booking Information**: Information related to your movie bookings, including selected movies, cinema locations, show times, selected seats, and booking history.
- **Communications**: Information you provide when contacting customer service.

### Information We Collect Automatically

When you use our service, we automatically collect:

- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with our app, including browsing behavior, movies viewed, features used, and time spent.
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and mobile device identifiers.
- **Location Data**: With your consent, we may collect your precise location to show nearby cinemas.
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities. See our Cookie Policy section for more details.

## Cookie Policy

### What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website or app. They allow us to recognize your device and remember your preferences.

### Types of Cookies We Use

#### Essential Cookies
Required for the operation of our service. They enable core functionality such as security, account authentication, and remembering your booking session. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

#### Functional Cookies
Allow us to remember choices you make and provide enhanced features. For example, remembering your preferred cinema location or language preferences.

#### Analytical Cookies
Help us understand how visitors interact with our service by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our platform.

#### Marketing Cookies
Track your browsing habits to enable us to show advertising which is more likely to be of interest to you. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement and help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

### Managing Cookies
Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may impact your overall user experience.

To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

### Do Not Track
Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. At this time, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.
